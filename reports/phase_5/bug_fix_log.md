## Verification boundaries

The successful tests establish CPU tensor contracts, finite-value handling, and gradient propagation with synthetic inputs. They do not establish emotion accuracy, valence accuracy, calibration, real-data behavior, pretrained-model integration, CUDA execution, or end-to-end production integration.
